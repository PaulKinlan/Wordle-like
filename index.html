Letterly

Tap the board and type with your keyboard

How to play

Guess the word in six tries. Tap the board and type with your phone's keyboard, then press Enter.

After each guess the tiles change colour:

  • P in the word and in the right spot
  • L in the word but in another spot
  • Y not in the word

Daily is one word a day, the same for everyone. Unlimited gives you a fresh word whenever you want.

Hard mode makes you use every hint you've found in later guesses.
