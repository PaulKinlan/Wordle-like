Letterle

Race your friends

Everyone gets the same word. When someone finishes a row, everyone else has 5 seconds to finish theirs, or that row is lost.

First to find the word wins. If nobody does, the best single row wins: 2 points for each green letter and 1 point for each yellow.

    How to play

    Guess the word in 6 tries. Tap the board and type with your phone's keyboard, then press Enter.

    After each guess the tiles change colour:

    • P in the word and in the right spot
    • L in the word but in another spot
    • Y not in the word

    Daily is one word a day, the same for everyone. Unlimited gives you a fresh word whenever you want.

    Hard mode picks a rarer word, and you must use every hint you've found in later guesses. It has its own daily word and its own stats.

    Race against friends: start a race, send the invite link, and everyone plays the same word. When someone finishes a row, the others have 5 seconds to finish theirs. First to find the word wins; if nobody does, the best single row wins (2 points per green, 1 per yellow).

    Other languages are in Settings. Accents are optional: É counts as E. Letters that are their own letter, like Spanish Ñ or German Ä, count separately.
